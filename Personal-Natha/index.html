Natha Janitra Adityi

My name is Natha, but people usually call me Nat or Natuy. I would describe myself as an ambivert because my personality depends on the situation and the people around me. Sometimes, I can easily socialize and start conversations with people I have just met, but there are also times when I prefer to stay quiet and observe. I also really enjoy dancing and singing, as well as reading books, especially Japanese thriller novels that involve mystery and unexpected plot twists.

Once I become close to someone, I can be very caring, talkative, and expressive. I am definitely the type of person who loves to yap about random things and share stories, even the smallest details. I enjoy having meaningful conversations, making memories, and spending time with people I feel comfortable with. I believe that every person has a different side to them, and I think mine comes out the most when I am surrounded by people I trust.
